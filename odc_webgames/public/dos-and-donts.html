Welcome to Maker Manners! 🎯

Test your knowledge of makerspace etiquette! In this challenge, you'll be presented with different scenarios that might happen in a makerspace. Your job is to decide whether each behavior is Polite or Rude.

How to Play:

  • Read each scenario carefully
  • Click "Polite ✅" if the behavior is appropriate
  • Click "Rude ❌" if the behavior is inappropriate
  • You'll get instant feedback with an explanation
  • Complete all 7 scenarios to see your final score!
